Illustration Fluid Rendering is a React SVG illustration for shadcn/ui that shows a browser card connected by colored lines to destination cards for platforms like Shopify, BigCommerce, and Contentful, with themeable colors and swappable icons. Use it in landing page sections about headless commerce or content rendering.
